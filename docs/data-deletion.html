Data Deletion Requests

Konjure Analytics does not collect or retain personal data from Meta platform users. The application posts its own published analysis to its own accounts; it does not request, read, or store other users' profile information, posts, or activity.

If you believe data relating to you is held, email rbingham1982@gmail.com with the subject Data Deletion Request. Requests are actioned within 30 days and confirmed by reply.
