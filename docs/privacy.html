Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Konjure Analytics ("we," "our," or "the service") publishes sports analytics — player projections, prop-market analysis and a weekly newsletter — for informational and entertainment purposes. This policy covers this website (konjureanalytics.com), the Konjure Analytics dashboard, the Konjure Analytics Android app, our newsletter, and our social media accounts. The short version: we collect only an email address and a password hash if you create an account, and we never sell or share them.

Information we collect

Accounts. Most of the service needs no account. The Edge Finder (the sportsbook boards and parlay builders) requires a free account. When you create one we store your email address, a one-way cryptographic hash of your password (scrypt with a unique random salt — we never store or see the password itself), and when the account was created and last used. These are kept in our database, hosted by Supabase, and used only to let you sign in. We do not ask for your name, phone number or location.

Saved parlay tickets. If you save a ticket in the parlay builder, we store it with your account's email address: the legs you chose, their odds and probabilities at the time, when you saved it, and how it was graded after the games. Tickets are used only to show you your own tickets and record, and are deleted with your account.

Konjure Pro payments. Konjure Pro subscriptions are processed by Stripe. You enter your payment details on Stripe's own checkout page; we never see or store your card number. Stripe shares with us only what we need to run your subscription: the email address on it and whether it is active. To check your access, we look up that email address in Stripe. Stripe keeps billing records under its own privacy policy and as the law requires.

Newsletter opt-in. The sign-up form has an unticked box to also receive our weekly newsletter. If you tick it, we record that choice on your account and pass your email address to beehiiv to subscribe you (see Our newsletter). If you leave it unticked, your email is not added to the newsletter.

We do not use analytics or advertising trackers. Neither this website nor the dashboard runs analytics, advertising or fingerprinting scripts of our own. Two third parties load only where you use them: the newsletter sign-up form on our homepage is served by beehiiv, and Konjure Pro checkout happens on Stripe's site. Each may set the cookies it needs to work, under its own privacy policy. The dashboard keeps you signed in for your browser session only.

Hosting logs. Like any website, the companies that host our pages record standard technical information when you visit — such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request — for security and operations. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and the dashboard on Streamlit Community Cloud; those logs are kept by those providers under their own privacy policies, not by us, and we do not use them to identify or profile visitors.

The Android app

The Konjure Analytics Android app displays the same dashboard available on the web. It requests only the permissions needed to load it over the internet (internet access and network state). It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files or device identifiers, and it does not collect or transmit any personal data of its own.

Our newsletter

If you choose to subscribe to our email newsletter — on beehiiv's signup page or with the opt-in box when creating an account — your email address is held by our newsletter provider, beehiiv, and used only to send you the newsletter. We do not sell or share it. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your subscription at any time (see Data deletion).

Social media and connected platforms

We post our own published analysis to our own accounts on Discord, Instagram, Threads and X. Where we connect to a platform's API (for example, Meta's APIs for Instagram and Threads), we use it only to publish to and manage our own accounts. We do not request, read, collect or store other users' profiles, posts, messages, followers or activity. If you interact with our posts, that interaction is governed by the platform's own privacy policy.

If you contact us

If you email us, we keep your message and email address only as long as needed to reply and to handle your request.

Data sources

Our analysis is built from publicly available sports and market data, which we retrieve from sources including the NBA and WNBA stats sites (stats.nba.com), the MLB Stats API, ESPN, nflverse, Sleeper, FanDuel, DraftKings, PrizePicks, Underdog Fantasy, The Odds API and Open-Meteo (weather). We send no information about you to any of them.

Data retention and deletion

We keep an account until you ask us to delete it. You can request deletion of your account, a newsletter subscription or an email you sent us at any time; see Data deletion. Requests are completed within 30 days and confirmed by reply, and deleting an account removes its email address, password hash and saved tickets from our database.

Children

Konjure Analytics discusses sports betting markets and is intended for adults (18 or older, and of legal age for sports wagering where you live). We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18; if you believe a minor has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Not betting advice

Everything we publish is model output and analysis for informational and entertainment purposes only. It is not betting, financial or investment advice, and no outcome is guaranteed.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the service handles information, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
