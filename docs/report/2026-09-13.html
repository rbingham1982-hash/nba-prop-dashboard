    The 5-leg parlay

    5 players across 5 different stat categories. The category spread is the only part of this that is defensible on its own terms: five legs from one market are close to the same bet five times over, and they price as independent when they are not.

    Touchdown scorers — Week 1

    Most likely to find the end zone, rushing or receiving. Passing touchdowns are excluded: a quarterback who throws one has not scored one.

    The model prices rushing and receiving touchdowns as Poisson counts, so a player fails to score only if both come up zero — `P = 1 - (1 - P_rush)(1 - P_rec)`.
