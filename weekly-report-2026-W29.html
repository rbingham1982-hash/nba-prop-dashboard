Closing line value

Now measurable, and mildly discouraging: MLB avg CLV −2.7 pts over 152 legs, WNBA −1.2 over 22 — the market has drifted slightly against the picks rather than toward them. Small, young sample, so not a verdict — but CLV is the metric to watch, since it converges in days instead of waiting on 5-leg variance.

What the full resolution revised

Resolving the whole backlog (this draft vs the first) moved three things:

  • ROI came down on 3–4× the sample: MLB recommended +116% → +74%, WNBA +10% → +4%. Still positive, less spectacular.
  • The same-game penalty was a mirage. The 0.72× correlation it was built on measured 1.34× (MLB) on the full data — no reliable effect — so the penalty is now inert (×1.0). One of the four new analytics does nothing on better data.
  • The market blend leans further to the market (MLB model share 0.51 → 0.32): with more data the model looks less sharp relative to the book.

Takeaway

The directional story survived the fuller data: profitable on 2-leg parlays, taxed by overconfident 5-leggers and combo props. But the honest update is that ROI is lower than the first cut, CLV is running slightly negative, and the same-game penalty didn't hold up — the market blend is the correction carrying real weight. Next week's blend-epoch results are the first true test.
